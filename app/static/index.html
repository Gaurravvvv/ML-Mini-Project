Applicant Input Field Guide

Complete reference for all 20 financial & demographic parameters used by the ML engine.

1. Loan Request & Credit Terms
Credit Amount (DM) Number (Positive Float)

Total loan principal amount requested in Deutsche Mark (or currency units).

What to enter: Any positive number (e.g. 2500, 10000, 50000, 100000). Typical loans range from 500 to 20,000 DM, but any amount is accepted.
Duration (Months) Number (Integer)

The scheduled loan repayment period / maturity term in months.

What to enter: Number of months (e.g. 12 for 1 yr, 24 for 2 yrs, 36 for 3 yrs, 48, 60). Longer terms increase default probability.
Installment Rate (% Income) Dropdown (Scale 1–4)

Installment payment size as a percentage of the applicant's monthly disposable income.

Options: 1 (1%), 2 (2%), 3 (3%), 4 (4%). Rate of 4 means a heavy debt service burden relative to income.
Loan Purpose Dropdown

Primary commercial or personal reason for borrowing.

Options: car (new), car (used), furniture/equipment, radio/television, domestic appliances, repairs, education, business, vacation, retraining, others.
Existing Credits at Bank Dropdown (Integer 1–4+)

Number of active, open credit lines or previous loans the applicant holds at this bank.

Options: 1, 2, 3, or 4 or more existing credits.
2. Financial Standing & Accounts
Checking Account Balance Dropdown (Key Driver)

Status of applicant's primary transactional checking account.

Options:
  • < 0 DM: Account is overdrawn / negative balance (highest risk indicator).
  • 0 - 200 DM: Modest positive balance.
  • >= 200 DM: Substantial balance or continuous salary deposits for ≥ 1 year.
  • no checking account: Customer has no checking account at this bank.
Savings Account / Bonds Dropdown (Key Driver)

Liquid financial reserves, savings accounts, or investment bonds.

Options: < 100 DM (Low cushion), 100 - 500 DM, 500 - 1000 DM, >= 1000 DM (Strong cushion), or unknown / no savings account.
Credit History Dropdown (Key Driver)

Historical track record of debt repayment and punctuality.

Options:
  • all credits at this bank paid duly: Flawless repayment history with this institution.
  • existing credits paid duly till now: Active loans paid on schedule.
  • delay in paying off in the past: Past record of delinquent/late payments.
  • critical account / other credits elsewhere: Heavily leveraged or problematic external accounts.
  • no credits taken / all paid duly: Clean debt history or first-time borrower.
Other Payment Plans Dropdown

Additional non-bank payment installment obligations or retail store charge accounts.

Options: none (no other payment plans), bank (installments at another bank), stores (retail store credit lines).
3. Personal & Socioeconomic Factors
Age (Years) Number (Years)

Applicant's biological age in completed years.

What to enter: Integer between 18 and 100+ (e.g. 25, 35, 48). Older applicants correlate with more established credit profiles.
Present Employment Dropdown

Continuous time working at current employer or business.

Options: unemployed, < 1 year, 1 - 4 years, 4 - 7 years, >= 7 years. Longer employment signals income stability.
Job Classification Dropdown

Professional role and skill qualification level.

Options: unemployed / unskilled non-resident, unskilled resident, skilled employee / official, management / self-employed / qualified.
Housing Status Dropdown

Residential living status.

Options: own (Homeowner), rent (Tenant paying rent), for free (Living with parents, family, or subsidized housing).
Property Ownership Dropdown

Highest value tangible asset owned that could serve as collateral.

Options: real estate, building society savings / life insurance, car or other, unknown / no property.
Personal Status / Sex Dropdown

Marital and family demographic status.

Options: male : single, male : married/widowed, female : single, female : divorced/separated/married, male : divorced/separated.
Residence Since (Years) Dropdown (Scale 1–4)

Number of continuous years lived at the current residential address.

Options: 1 year, 2 years, 3 years, 4+ years.
Other Debtors / Guarantors Dropdown

Third parties who co-sign or legally guarantee the credit obligation.

Options: none (individual loan), co-applicant (joint borrower), guarantor (external party backing repayment).
Dependents Dropdown

Number of persons financially dependent on the applicant for maintenance.

Options: 1 dependent or 2 or more.
Foreign Worker Dropdown

Whether the applicant is working as a foreign national.

Options: Yes or No.
4. Prediction Models
Random Forest Classifier Bagging Ensemble

Trains 150 randomized decision trees. Captures non-linear interactions across demographic and financial attributes without overfitting. Recommended default.

Logistic Regression Linear Log-Odds

Standard regulatory compliant model that outputs direct log-odds coefficients and well-calibrated probabilities. Achieves high sensitivity (80% recall on defaulters).

AdaBoost Classifier Boosting Ensemble

Sequentially trains adaptive decision stumps that iteratively focus weight on borderline / hard-to-classify borrowers.
